# News verified for 17 November 2026

Researched 18 Nov 2026 (WIB) from BBC world headlines, One Turn News'
17 Nov digest, Hansen Intel, Foreign Exchanges' world roundup, NPR's
Up First (17 Nov), and wire reporting. Each story below appeared in at
least one major outlet's 17 November 2026 coverage.

1. **U.S.–Iran exchange fire in the Gulf.** U.S. jets struck an Iranian
   missile launch site on Qeshm Island; Iran fired drones at a U.S. radar
   at Sheikh Isa base in Bahrain — its third attack on Bahrain in five
   days. Trump said the U.S. was "looking into" a reported Iranian
   dirty-bomb plot; Iran denies it.
2. **Kerch Bridge partly collapses.** Ukrainian drones struck the bridge
   to Crimea for a second day; a road section fell into the strait. Kyiv
   says the operation took weeks to plan.
3. **Trump's Asia trip.** The U.S. president arrived in Manila for the
   ASEAN summit and heads to Shenzhen, China, for APEC talks with Xi
   Jinping and Vladimir Putin; Ukraine on the agenda.
4. **Nvidia: record $115bn+ quarter, Huang stays out.** Third-quarter
   revenue above $115 billion, about double a year earlier; the firm says
   ousted CEO Jensen Huang will not be reinstated.
5. **Ebola in DR Congo passes 11,000 cases.** Cases more than doubled in
   three months; 5,400 deaths reported (UN News).
6. **Gaza aid call.** The UK, Australia, Canada and Italy jointly urged
   Israel to open crossings for humanitarian aid, calling the situation
   catastrophic; Britain sanctioned eight Israeli settlement builders.
7. **POW exchange.** Zelenskyy announced a large prisoner exchange
   (1,000+ each side) after trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi.
8. **Houthis hit Saudi pipeline.** Drones targeted Saudi Arabia's
   East-West oil pipeline as Houthi forces advanced toward Aden; the
   Yemeni government pulled troops back from the Red Sea coast.
9. **U.S. loses measles elimination status.** After 26 years, amid
   5,400+ cases — a 40-year high — and 10 deaths.
10. **Voyager 1 reaches one light-day.** NASA's probe, launched 1977, is
    now over 16 billion miles from Earth.
11. **France strikes.** 146,000 protested across France (28,000 in
    Paris) as unions struck over the cost of living.
12. **Russian military plane crash.** An aircraft crashed in Kemerovo
    region, killing all 19 on board; technical malfunction suspected.
